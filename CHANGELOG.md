# Changelog

Every release needs a section here: the release build takes its notes from it, and the app shows
them under Settings → Updates.

## 0.8.1 — 2026-09-25
- The tray flyout's level meters are exactly as wide as the sliders above them

## 0.8.0 — 2026-09-25
- Settings → Updates shows what changed in every version, and what's new in an available update
- The tray flyout's Open mixer button is dark instead of white
- Faster release builds

## 0.7.3 — 2026-09-25
- The tray flyout's level meters are dotted again, like the Mixer's

## 0.7.2 — 2026-09-25
- The Mixer's dotted level meters are back

## 0.7.1 — 2026-09-25
- Fixed sound lagging behind video after a Bluetooth speaker reconnects or the output device changes
- Audio that piles up while the output is away is dropped within half a second instead of slowly catching up

## 0.7.0 — 2026-09-25
- Measure the full delay from an app to your output device in Settings → Devices
- Click the Buffer pill in the Mixer to open the delay measurement

## 0.6.0 — 2026-09-25
- Redesigned Mixer, Apps and Microphone views
- The microphone's limiter can be switched off
- New gear icon; Settings sits in a centred column

## 0.5.2 — 2026-09-25
- Updates install silently, without the installer window

## 0.5.1 — 2026-09-25
- Smowaudio never remembers a VB-Cable as your own Windows default device

## 0.5.0 — 2026-09-25
- Updates download without a token

## 0.4.1 — 2026-09-24
- Updates come from the public Smowaudio repository

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-24
- Redesigned Settings with Devices, General, Shortcuts and Updates tabs
- Redesigned tray flyout with a status pill, channel and mic panels

## 0.3.3 — 2026-09-24
- Level meters on the Apps cards are visible and even in dark mode

## 0.3.2 — 2026-09-24
- Clean segmented meters in the tray flyout

## 0.3.1 — 2026-09-24
- The bottom of the tray flyout is no longer cut off

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-24
- In-app updates from GitHub releases

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-24
- Slow actions run in the background so the window never freezes
- The mixer and flyout update in place when something changes elsewhere
- Microphone test: record a few seconds and play them back
- More precise app level meters
- Tray flyout sliders match the Mixer's

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-17
- Game, Chat, Media and Aux channels over VB-Cable, with a master output and EQ
- Microphone chain with noise removal, gate, EQ, compressor and limiter
- Sets Windows default devices like Sonar and follows device changes
- Apps view with each app's own icon; drag apps between channels
- Tray flyout with an output picker, channel mutes and volume sliders (double-click resets to 100 %)
- Global keyboard shortcuts for almost every setting, with an on-screen overlay; hold a volume shortcut to glide
